How It Works

Detecting plant diseases has never been easier. Just three simple steps to secure your crop health.

1

Upload Image

Take a picture of the affected chilli leaf using your phone camera or upload an existing image from your gallery directly to our secure dashboard.

2

AI Analysis

Our custom Deep Learning model processes the image in real-time, analyzing cellular and structural damage to identify the exact pathogen.

3

Get Actionable Results

Receive a detailed diagnosis including severity, confidence scores, and step-by-step organic treatment plans to save your plant.

Comprehensive Disease Coverage

Our model is specifically trained on a high-quality, preprocessed dataset to detect the most critical chilli crop threats with over 90% accuracy.

Whitefly & Leaf Curl

Instantly detect Whitefly infestations and the devastating Leaf Curl Virus they transmit. Catch it early before it spreads to the entire crop.

Fungal Anthracnose

Identify fungal spots (Anthracnose) causing fruit rot. Receive immediate organic fungicide recommendations to salvage your yield.

Nutrient Deficiencies

Diagnose Yellowish (chlorosis) symptoms often caused by nutrient deficiencies or watering issues, and get precise soil adjustment tips.
